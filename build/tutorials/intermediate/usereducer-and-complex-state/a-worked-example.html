Note

Notice that visible is calculated during render rather than stored in state. Filters, totals and counts are almost always derived - keeping them in the reducer just creates another thing to keep in sync.

Where async work goes

Reducers stay pure, so requests happen in event handlers or effects, and their results are dispatched as actions.
